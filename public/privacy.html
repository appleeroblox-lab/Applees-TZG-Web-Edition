Privacy Policy

The Zero Gravity Web Edition · Meowple Studios
Updated November 14, 2026

Our game uses account information to provide sign-in, saved progress and social features.

Information we use

We store your profile details, birthday, game progress, messages and reports when you use those features. Google sign-in provides your Google account identifier, name, email address and profile photo. We use these to authenticate your account and help set up your profile.

Storage and sharing

Supabase handles our account authentication and cloud data. Our hosting providers, Cloudflare and GitHub Pages, process requests needed to serve the website. Your browser stores session and game settings locally.

Your public profile and global chat messages can be seen by other players. Direct and group messages are shared with their recipients. Authorized moderators may review account information and reports to provide support and enforce our rules. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can edit your profile and request account deletion through Account settings or our contact email. We retain account data while your account is active; moderation reports may remain after deletion where needed for safety or legal obligations. You can also revoke our Google access in your Google Account settings.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as our game changes. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact us using the email below.
