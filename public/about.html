The Zero Gravity Web Edition

Explore space together in The Zero Gravity Web Edition. Multiplayer Beta adds real-time multiplayer, public servers, and shared space exploration in your browser.

Game features

  • Real-time multiplayer in Multiplayer Beta with synchronized ships and on-foot players.
  • Public servers for flying and exploring together.
  • Shared space exploration across planetary surfaces and star systems.
  • Single-player exploration, planetary landings, mining, and crafting.

How to join a public server

Sign in to the game, then choose Play, Multiplayer, and Public Servers. Choose a server to join or use Quick Join.
