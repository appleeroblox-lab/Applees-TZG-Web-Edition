Texture credits & scientific notes

Solar System surfaces use downloaded image maps. Exoplanets and simulated worlds use fictional game textures. These are artistic surfaces, not telescope photographs of exoplanets.

Solar System Scope / INOVE

Solar System Scope textures, based on NASA imagery and elevation data, licensed under CC BY 4.0. Credit: Solar System Scope / INOVE. Some original maps fill unobserved gaps with fictional terrain and adjust colors. Maps are wrapped onto 3D spheres.

Included: Earth day, Moon, Sun, Milky Way star panorama, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune. Earth, Moon, Sun and stars are the downsampled copies distributed by homer-jay. Other planet files are mirrored by Sanghel/solar-system-3d. Reusing the Sun map on other stars is an illustration, not an observed stellar surface. Unmapped minor moons use explicitly procedural placeholders; the Earth Moon map is no longer reused on them. Dwarf planets without their own map still use borrowed illustrative terrain.

Moon image mosaics

18 body-specific spacecraft mosaics: Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, Rhea, Iapetus, Miranda, Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Oberon, Triton, Phobos, and Deimos. Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech/USGS; Voyager, Galileo and Viking missions. Downloaded from the JPL Solar System Simulator map archive. These older mosaics can have gaps, grayscale regions or aesthetic adjustments. Exact source files and credits. Other minor moons have labeled procedural placeholders, rather than the Earth Moon texture.

Imagined exoplanet surfaces

Planet Surface Textures by Screaming Brain Studios, CC0 / Public Domain. Selected maps: Arid_01, Grassland_01 and Snowy_01, 1024 × 512. The original file names are preserved here as credits; filenames in the game are exo5.png, exo6.png and exo7.png. These artistic textures do not represent measured exoplanet topography. Pack license.

Black holes and distant destinations

Black-hole disks, bent light arcs and nebula volumes are generated effects. They do not solve general relativity. See NASA’s black-hole visualization for the scientific inspiration. Sagittarius A*, M87*, Cygnus X-1 and V404 Cygni are named destinations shown at illustrative size and spacing. No landing is allowed.

TRAPPIST-1 planets are known worlds, but their surfaces remain imagined. Discovery paper. Solar System orbital radii are approximate and phases illustrative. Other star/galaxy positions and sizes are scaled for this sandbox. Warp is fictional and time-compressed.

Mining simulation

All mineral placement, resource abundance, sale values and resource HP are game simulations. Real mineral names do not imply confirmed deposits at these locations. The exotic resources on imagined worlds are fictional.

Additional spacecraft maps · v16

Pluto: NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI, New Horizons MVIC. Charon: NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute, New Horizons LORRI. Titan: NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute, Cassini ISS near-infrared; map frame cropped. Ceres: NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA, Dawn enhanced-color clear-filter mosaic. Coverage gaps and enhanced colors remain in these maps. Source pages and notes are in textures/EXTENDED_SOURCES.json.

Makemake, Eris, Haumea and dwarf-planet candidates have distinct surface illustrations; they are not resolved spacecraft maps. The simulated external-galaxy sky panoramas are original seeded illustrations, not photographs taken inside those galaxies. Solar and nearby-star scenes use the credited ESO Milky Way panorama.

Surface colors, stars and black-hole effects

Local landscapes are procedural representations. Broad color families follow spacecraft observations, rather than surveyed landing sites. Unobserved exoplanet surfaces remain artistic.

  • NASA: Mercury approximate true color
  • NASA: Mars surface colors
  • NASA/JPL: Io sulfur and frost colors
  • NASA: Europa imagery
  • ESO: Proxima and Alpha Centauri star colors
  • NASA: accretion disks and Doppler beaming

The black-hole effects are animated artistic disk and photon-ring effects, rather than a general-relativity ray-tracing simulation.

Applee-selected Roblox surface artwork

The active maps for 46 named worlds use the asset IDs supplied by Applee. Public Roblox preview images are bundled locally at up to 420 pixels wide; these are not the full-resolution originals. Assets and download dimensions are listed in the texture manifest. These selections supersede the earlier bundled maps for those worlds. No claim is made that the selected artwork is an observed surface mosaic. Planet X is explicitly hypothetical; added sizes, phases and unobserved terrain are game illustrations.
